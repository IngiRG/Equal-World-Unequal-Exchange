EMPIRICAL SPECIFICATION v0.1

A counterfactual that removes a specified hierarchy, not every difference.

The baseline is a static Equal World. It conditions on the supplied productive structure and asks what remuneration and production prices would look like if equivalent effective-labor claims received equivalent remuneration while the global real remuneration pool were conserved.

1 · Real remuneration

wᴿᵢ = wᴺᵢ / qᵢ

EXIOBASE compensation is first converted from EUR to local currency using market exchange rates, then divided by the country PPP factor. EWA is constructed in purchasing-power space. After equal-world real remuneration is calculated, it is converted back through PPP and the exchange rate before entering the MRIO price system. PPP therefore permits equal-world nominal wages to differ across countries even when real equivalent claims are equal.

2 · Equivalent claims

uᴬᵢ = wᴿᵢ / eᵢ

The effective-labor coefficient e is a retained conditioning characteristic in the static model. Holding it fixed is not a claim that observed productivity differences are morally legitimate or historically exogenous.

3 · Aggregate conservation

ū* = Σ(wᴿᵢLᵢ) / Σ(eᵢLᵢ)

The Equal World does not assign everyone a Northern wage. It redistributes the observed global real remuneration pool under the declared equivalence rule.

4 · Equal World wage

w*ᵢ = ū*eᵢ

5 · Production prices

p* = (I − A)⁻¹v*

The production release must expose the construction of v*, including labor coefficients and every normal-return assumption. No distributive parameter should be hidden in the visualization layer.

6 · Hierarchy gap

H = V* − Vᴬ

Trade quantities are held fixed in the static exercise. A positive gap means the fixed bundle commands more under the specified Equal World. A one-period gap is not, by itself, evidence sufficient to establish imperialism.

Interpretation boundary

This baseline does not undo historical effects on technology, capital stocks, productivity or sector structure. Dynamic and historical Equal Worlds are separate estimands and should be reported separately.
